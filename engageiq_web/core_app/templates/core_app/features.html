Predictive Feature Vectors

EngageIQ evaluates employee retention risk through four highly correlation-optimized parameters.

📈 1. Compensation Weights

Monitors salary structures against standard market valuations to identify compensation-driven churn indicators.

🎯 2. Career Progression

Calculates velocity scores based on promotional intervals and specialized training track options.

⚖️ 3. Work-Life Boundary

Tracks burnout risk matrices based on extra project task allocations and structural overtime trends.

🤝 4. Managerial Connection

Applies satisfaction scores based on structured survey reviews and manager-to-subordinate dynamics.
